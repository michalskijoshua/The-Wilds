## What exists and works (server-tested; most of it also played on Android)

### Tuskbeast (the main creature)
- **Wild and rare:** at most **one per 750 × 750-block square**. It appears the first time a player is in a matching biome in that square, 32–40 blocks away. Once tamed or killed, the square can get a new one after **3 real days**.
- **Three kinds by biome:**
  - Savanna: `savanna`, `savanna_plateau`, `savanna_mutated`, `savanna_plateau_mutated`
  - Badlands: Bedrock's `mesa`, `mesa_bryce`, `mesa_plateau`, `mesa_plateau_mutated`, `mesa_plateau_stone`, `mesa_plateau_stone_mutated`
  - Tundra: `ice_plains`, `ice_plains_spikes`, `ice_mountains`

  Spawn eggs also take the kind of the biome they're used in.
- **Taming:** borrowed from the horse (`minecraft:tamemount`). Ride it until it stops throwing you off, or feed it: hay bale +15, golden apple +10, golden carrot +5, wheat/apple/sugar +3. The rider who tames it becomes the **owner**.
- **Tuskbeast menu:** sneak + tap with an empty hand. It replaced the Howdah Flag item, which was removed.
  - Owner: **Build a Howdah (20 emeralds, in Creative too)**, Climb aboard with my guards, Send guards down, Riders (invite/remove).
  - Invited players: Climb aboard with my guards.
  - Wild beast: a taming hint.
- **Before the howdah:** one seat on the neck (the driver steers); no chest, no tower.
- **With a howdah:**
  - The neck driver seat plus **4 passenger seats** in the howdah corners, for invited players or the riders' own Guilds+ **archers and crossbowmen** (only those two kinds board; they sit).
  - An invisible **seat keeper** holds the neck seat when only guards are aboard, so guards never drive.
  - A **16-slot chest**: open your inventory while driving.
  - **The howdah tower** shoots for the crew: each archer adds 1 arrow every 1.5 s, each crossbowman 1 bolt every 2.5 s (faster, +2 damage). Shots go at the nearest hostile mob in sight within 24 blocks, use arrows from the chest, never shoot through a player, and never hurt riders. The guards' own Guilds+ attacks are left alone.
- **Invites:** typed `/wilds:invite <name>`, `/wilds:uninvite <name>`, `/wilds:invites` (no cheats needed), or the Riders menu. An invite covers all of the owner's beasts. Uninvited players are put down.
- **Durability:** 300 health, all damage × 0.4, no fall damage. **Wounded** at ≤ 25 % health (slow, plays the Wounded clips), recovers at 40 %. Heals slowly after 20 s without damage.
- **Steps up through snow** (auto-step 1.56 blocks).
- **Models:** Codex v2.0, 3 kinds × unarmoured/armoured, 17 clips each, including kneel and Wounded. They're turned 180° in game (Codex's heads point +Z). Looks are switched with vanilla queries only (`q.mark_variant` = kind, `q.variant` = howdah, `q.skin_id` = Wounded), generated by `tools/tusk_looks.py`.

### Pebble Mimic
- 10 forms (Codex/Director models): stone, mossy, sand, clay, coal, copper, iron, gold, diamond, netherite.
- Affinity is rolled once at birth: common 75 % / uncommon 20 % / rare 4 % / diamond 0.9 % / netherite 0.1 %.
- It changes form by touching a block of that material, only up to its affinity tier.
- Two sizes: large = half the full model (≈2 blocks wide), small = half a large one.
- Clips used: idle, crawl, hit, death. The disguise/boulder clips aren't used yet.

### Aetherseed
- 3 forms: original, bloom, withered, with a glow layer. It hovers 1–2 blocks up (allay-style) at scale 0.25.
- Every 45 s it pulses, radius 5, at most 3 plants:
  - Original: crops grow.
  - Bloom: saplings ripen, flowers spread.
  - Withered: crops shrink, flowers and saplings die.
- Flower pots are never touched.

### Landmarks (giants)
- Fixed 1,500-block cells.
  - Oni Cairn: cherry grove only.
  - Meadow Colossus: 50 % of cells.
  - Serpentwreath: 15 %.
  - Stormmantle: 35 %.
- Placed once per cell, never duplicated. Felled giants return after **3 real days**.
- **Still placeholder marker cubes** with a floating name.

- Riding guards skip patrols and supply trips.
- The Commander's Horn "Return to posts" makes riders get off.
- Arrows fired for a riding guard don't use his own arrows or wear his bow.
- **Proposed, not built (part 4 of `GUILDS_PATCH_PROPOSAL.md`):**

  - Guilds+ has no members or wars today. The Wilds side is already built and idle.

## Lessons that must be kept (Android)
- **No `q.attack_time`** in client files: it makes mobs invisible on Android.
- **No entity properties for looks:** use vanilla `q.variant` / `q.mark_variant` / `q.skin_id`.
- Removing a component group strips its components outright: swap groups in pairs.
- No `minecraft:inventory` on fighting mobs. A `ranged_attack` with speed 0 never shoots.
- **Bedrock biome ids differ from Java** (`mesa`, `ice_plains`, `extreme_hills`…).
- Several models on one entity must not have bones named with "neck" at different spots (`armor_offset.default_neck` clash).
- Codex models face +Z, so turn them 180° in game.
- The Director reads errors from the **Content Log** on the phone (Settings > Creator) and sends screenshots.

## Testing tools (for whoever builds)
- `tools/bds_install.sh` / `bds_run.sh` / `bds_send.sh`: a headless Bedrock server; commands go through a file.
- Wilds test hooks: `/scriptevent wilds:test <cmd> x y z [args]`. Commands include:
  - Tuskbeast: `seat`, `tame`, `howdah`, `report`, `tower`, `looks`
  - wild spawning: `wild_visit`, `wild_age`
  - Mimic and Aetherseed: `forms`, `mimic_*`, `seed`, `garden`, `garden_state`
  - other: `invite_check`, `biomes`
- Generators: `tools/tusk_looks.py` (Tuskbeast looks), `tools/wilds_models.py` (Blockbench → Bedrock for the Mimic and Aetherseed, using `tools/bbmodel_convert.py` copied from Guilds+).
- History: `CHANGELOG.md`; test results: `PHASE0_REPORT.md`.
